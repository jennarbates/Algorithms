Algorithms, seen

One interactive page per algorithm, built over a semester. Each explains the real procedure to someone with no maths background, without simplifying it into something untrue. Press the buttons, break the results, read the proofs when you want them.

No tracking, no dependencies, one file per page • Source and notes on GitHub

The shelf

  • Stable matching Live

    Who gets in, and why

    Three students, three schools, one seat each. Watch who asks whom, who gets held, who gets bumped, and then try to find two people who would rather have each other. The result depends on who does the asking.

    Gale-Shapley, 1962 Open
  • Growth of functions Live

    The Big-O Building

    A running time stands on a roof. Its small terms jump off, and what is left up top is c times a simpler function. Then watch that function try to get back up without c, and end up in the basement, which turns out to be a floor. Ten scenes, with the real curves alongside.

    T(n) ≤ c·f(n) for n ≥ n₀ Open
  • Growth of functions Live

    Floors and ceilings

    Big-O's other half. Set c and n₀ yourself and watch a floor hold or break, exactly, down to the n where it gives. Then count Print1 and sum-product line by line, and see where polynomial time ends.

    c₁·f(n) ≤ T(n) ≤ c₂·f(n) Open
  • Graph traversal Live

    How to walk a graph

    The 1970 Internet, searched from MIT until it falls into layers. Then the one word between breadth first and depth first, a two-colour test that draws its own proof, and courses to put in an order you choose. Four chapters, four tiers of practice.

    BFS, DFS, bipartite, topological sort Open
